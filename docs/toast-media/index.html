Buffering.simulation

(Not really) simulating the feeling of waiting video to load. A run-in-your-browser-tab app that streaming some video on a disk and serving some image on... a disk... as well.

Basically

This is an attempt to solve my own frustration of (some of) bloated anime streaming site and large titled manga site (mangadex) just randomly being DMCA's out of no where one day.

After you obtain all of your favorite shows, you do want to display it somehow. So, this project was born. Simple as that.
